What HR does every day, without the spreadsheets

Employee records

Keep job title, department, salary and hire date for every person, and find anyone in seconds with search.

Departments

See how many people work in each team and who leads it. Rename a team and everyone moves with it.

Leave approvals

Staff request time off from their own login. You see what's waiting and approve or reject in one click.

Attendance

One tap to clock in and out. Arrivals after 09:15 are flagged as late, so you never have to count by hand.

One system, two views

HR administrators

Add and edit employees, manage departments, approve leave, see monthly payroll and check everyone's attendance.

Employees

Browse the staff directory, request leave, track the answer and clock in and out. HR creates their login when adding them.
